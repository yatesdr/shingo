Stations

One row per enrolled edge station. The name is yours to change — it is read by nothing but a person, it never travels on the wire, and renaming updates every screen including past orders, because no stored row holds it. The uid is the identity everything is keyed on; it is fixed at enrollment and is not editable here or anywhere else.

An edge that has never been configured mints its own uid and comes up working — it appears here as Unclaimed. That is a question, not a fault: the box has said it exists, and nobody has said what it is. Name it and it becomes an ordinary station.
If it is REPLACING a station that already exists, do not name it. Put the existing station's uid on that box instead — from the list below, or by restoring its backup — so its history stays attached, then delete the unclaimed row. Naming it would leave the old station's history under one uid and everything after the swap under another.
