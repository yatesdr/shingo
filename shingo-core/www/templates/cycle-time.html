What is being measured. The interval between consecutive ticks for the same part at the same station. Edge accumulates deltas and flushes them every {{.FlushInterval}}, so that is the resolution of every figure here and nothing finer is printed. A median at or below it is measuring the transport rather than the cell, and its row says so.

Station, not node. The applied-delta audit row carries no node — the writer populates neither node_id nor the station column and passes the station as the actor — so the grain here is station, part and direction. Attributing these to a node needs a change at the ingest path, not at this page.

Percentiles are order statistics, not interpolations. Every figure printed is an interval that actually occurred. Bands are relative to each key’s own median so a 25-second line and a 75-second line can be read on one axis, and they are tolerance ranges — never equality, never whole-second buckets.
